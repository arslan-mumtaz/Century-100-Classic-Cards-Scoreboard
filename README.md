# Century-100-Classic-Cards-Scoreboard
Privacy Policy for Century 100
Effective Date: September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Century 100: Classic Cards & Scoreboard mobile application developed by Mehdi Technologies ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information We Collect
We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).

No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use Century 100.
Local Gameplay Data: Scores, player names, and round history are stored locally on your device storage.
Multiplayer Rooms: When joining an online multiplayer room, temporary session room data (such as player alias and room code) is used strictly to sync active card game moves between players in real-time.
2. Permissions Used
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely to facilitate real-time multiplayer room card synchronization and cloud leaderboard fetching.
VIBRATE: Used for tactile in-game feedback during card interactions.
3. Third-Party Services
The app connects to secure real-time backend infrastructure solely to facilitate peer-to-peer multiplayer room gameplay. No personal advertising trackers or third-party profiling data brokers are utilized.

4. No Gambling / Real-Money Gaming
Century 100 is a traditional points-based trick/rummy style casual card and scorekeeper utility game. The app does not involve real-money betting, gambling, financial wagers, or payouts of any monetary value.

5. Children's Privacy
Century 100 does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The game contains family-friendly content suitable for general audiences.
